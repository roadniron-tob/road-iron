# Road & Iron Log — install on your phone

Everything you log stays on your phone. Photo scanning, typed meals, watch screenshots and the coach use Google's Gemini AI (free API key). Barcodes are looked up in Open Food Facts, no key needed.

## 1. Put the app online (one time, about 10 minutes, free)

1. On your phone, open this ZIP in **My Files** and tap **Extract**.
2. In Chrome, go to **github.com** and create a free account.
3. Tap **+** → **New repository**. Name it `road-iron`, leave it **Public**, tap **Create repository**.
4. On the new repository page, tap **uploading an existing file**, choose all the files from the extracted folder (index.html, sw.js, manifest.webmanifest, the three icon PNGs, README.md) and tap **Commit changes**.
   - If you don't see the upload option, tap Chrome's **⋮** → **Desktop site** and try again.
5. Go to **Settings** → **Pages**. Under **Branch**, pick **main** and **/(root)**, tap **Save**.
6. Wait 1–2 minutes. Your app is at `https://YOUR-GITHUB-NAME.github.io/road-iron/`

The app's code is public, but your log, photos and API key are not. They never leave your phone (except what you send to Google AI).

## 2. Install it

Open that address in Chrome and tap **Install app** at the top (or **⋮** → **Add to Home screen**). Open it from the new icon from now on.

## 3. Turn on Google AI

1. Go to **aistudio.google.com/apikey**, sign in with Google, tap **Create API key**, copy it.
2. In the app: **Guide** tab → **Google AI setup** → paste the key → **Save & test**.

## 4. Back up

Guide tab → **Save backup file** every week or two, and before changing phones. Keep a copy in Google Drive.
